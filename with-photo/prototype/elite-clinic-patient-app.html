Elite ClinicPatient App

What the patient carries home

The other half of the clinic app — the same product, from the man's side.

  • Tap the bottom bar for Home, Visits, Records and Messages.
  • Open the menu for medications, invoices, care plan, consents and Elite Passage.
  • Tap Book a visit and take it through to the confirmation.
  • Open a blood result — reference ranges, what moved, and what the consultant said.
  • Turn on Discreet mode in the menu to see the privacy blur.
Prototype. Every name, result, date and price is invented. Nothing is saved and nothing connects to a real system.
