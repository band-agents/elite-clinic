Elite ClinicClinic App

The app the clinic runs on

A working prototype, not a slideshow. Drive it like a real phone.

  • Tap the bottom bar to move between Today, Bookings, Patients and Results.
  • Open the menu (top left) for payments, messages, rota, reports and settings.
  • Tap ＋ New booking and take it all the way to the confirmation.
  • Tap any blurred name to reveal it — discreet mode, on by default.
Prototype. Every name, result and price is invented for the demo. Nothing is saved and nothing connects to a real system.
